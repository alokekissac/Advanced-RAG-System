# Annual Leave and Time Off Policy

> Synthetic demo document for a fictional company.

## Annual leave entitlement

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to Irish public holidays. After five years of continuous service the entitlement rises to 27 days.

Part-time employees receive leave pro rata to their contracted hours.

## Booking leave

Leave is requested in the HR portal (PeopleDesk). Requests of five or more consecutive working days must be submitted at least four weeks in advance. Shorter requests need one week's notice.

Managers must respond to a leave request within three working days.

## Carrying over leave

Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March, after which they are forfeited. Carry-over above 5 days requires written approval from the Head of People and is only granted in exceptional circumstances, such as a delayed project launch.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 10 days of fully paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences of more than three consecutive days. Sick leave is reported to your manager by 09:30 on the first day of absence.

## Parental and family leave

Halcyon tops up statutory maternity benefit to full salary for 26 weeks. Paternity leave is 4 weeks at full salary. Parent's leave follows the statutory entitlement.

## Volunteering days

Every employee may take 2 paid volunteering days per year for a registered charity.

## Company closure

The company closes between 24 December and 1 January. These days are not deducted from annual leave.
