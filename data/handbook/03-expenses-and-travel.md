# Expenses and Business Travel

> Synthetic demo document for a fictional company.

## Submitting expenses

Expenses are submitted in the Ledgerly app within 30 days of the purchase, with a photo of the itemised receipt. Claims submitted later than 60 days after purchase are not reimbursed.

Approved expenses are paid with the next monthly payroll run, provided they were approved before the 20th of the month.

## Approval limits

- Up to €250: approved by your line manager.
- €250 to €2,000: approved by your department head.
- Above €2,000: approved by the Chief Financial Officer and requires a purchase order raised in advance.

## Meals and subsistence

When travelling for work, the daily meal allowance is €60 in Ireland and €75 abroad. Alcohol is never reimbursed.

## Flights and trains

Economy class is standard for all flights. Premium economy is allowed for flights longer than 6 hours. Flights must be booked through the travel desk (TravelPoint) at least 14 days before departure where possible.

Train travel is preferred over flying for journeys under 4 hours.

## Hotels

The nightly hotel cap is €180 in Ireland and €220 in the rest of Europe, including breakfast. Field Operations staff working at remote wind farm sites may exceed the cap by up to 20% when no compliant hotel is available within 30 km of the site.

## Mileage

Use of a personal car is reimbursed at €0.40 per km for the first 1,500 km in a calendar year, and €0.25 per km after that.

## Corporate cards

Corporate cards are issued to employees who travel at least monthly. Personal purchases on a corporate card are a disciplinary matter.
