# Pay and Benefits

> Synthetic demo document for a fictional company.

## Pay dates

Salaries are paid monthly on the 25th. If the 25th falls on a weekend or public holiday, pay is issued on the previous working day.

Salaries are reviewed once a year in April, based on the performance review and market benchmarking.

## Pension

Halcyon runs a defined contribution pension scheme. The company matches employee contributions up to 6% of basic salary. Employees are enrolled automatically after their first month and may opt out within 3 months.

## Health insurance

Private health insurance is provided for employees and their dependants, with the company paying 100% of the employee's premium and 50% of dependants' premiums.

## Bonus

The annual bonus is based on company results and individual performance, with a target of 8% of basic salary. Bonuses are paid in the June payroll. Employees who joined after 1 October are not eligible for that year's bonus.

## Bike to work

Halcyon participates in the government Cycle to Work scheme, with a limit of €1,250 for a standard bicycle and €1,500 for an e-bike.

## Employee assistance programme

A free, confidential counselling service is available 24/7 to employees and their families on 1800 555 010.
