# New Starter Onboarding Guide

> Synthetic demo document for a fictional company.

## Before day one

Your laptop is shipped to your home address five working days before your start date. Your manager assigns an onboarding buddy from another team.

## Your first week

- Day 1: IT setup at 09:30, SSO enrolment and hardware security key collection.
- Day 1: welcome lunch with your team.
- Day 2: health and safety induction. It is mandatory before visiting any field site or the test hangar.
- Day 3 to 5: meetings with each department's onboarding lead.

## Probation

The probation period is 6 months. Probation reviews happen at 3 months and at 6 months. Probation can be extended once, by up to 3 months.

## Mandatory training

The following courses must be completed in the Learning Hub within 30 days of joining:

1. Information security awareness
2. Data protection (GDPR) essentials
3. Anti-harassment and respect at work
4. Drone flight safety basics (for all staff, not just pilots)

## Learning budget

Every employee has a personal learning budget of €1,500 per year for courses, conferences and books. Unused budget does not carry over.

## Buddy programme

Buddies meet new starters weekly for the first 8 weeks.
