# Hybrid and Remote Work Policy

> Synthetic demo document for a fictional company.

## Hybrid pattern

Halcyon works in a hybrid pattern. Employees in office-based roles are expected in the office at least 2 days per week, one of which is the team's anchor day set by the department head.

Field Operations staff follow their site rota instead of the hybrid pattern.

## Working from abroad

Employees may work from another EU country for up to 20 working days per calendar year, subject to manager approval. Working from outside the EU is not permitted because of data protection and tax rules. Requests are made in PeopleDesk at least two weeks in advance.

## Home office allowance

New employees receive a one-off home office allowance of €400 for a desk, chair or monitor. Receipts are submitted through Ledgerly. In addition, employees receive a remote working payment of €3.20 per day worked from home, as allowed under Irish Revenue rules.

## Equipment

Every employee receives a laptop, a 27-inch monitor and a headset. Additional equipment is requested from the IT service desk.

## Internet and security at home

Work from home must use a secured Wi-Fi network (WPA2 or better). Public Wi-Fi may only be used with the company VPN switched on.
