# IT and Information Security Policy

> Synthetic demo document for a fictional company.

## Passwords and authentication

All company accounts use single sign-on (SSO) with multi-factor authentication. Hardware security keys are mandatory for Engineering and for anyone with production access. Passwords, where still used, must be at least 14 characters and are stored only in the company password manager (Vaultline).

Never share an MFA code with anyone, including IT. IT staff will never ask for your code.

## Laptops

Company laptops use full-disk encryption and must lock after 5 minutes of inactivity. Laptops are replaced every 4 years.

Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT service desk within 1 hour of discovery, by calling +353 1 555 0199 or emailing security@halcyon.example.

## Data classification

Halcyon classifies information into four levels:

1. Public: marketing material and published papers.
2. Internal: handbook, org charts, most documents.
3. Confidential: customer contracts, financials, unreleased designs.
4. Restricted: flight telemetry from customer sites and personal data.

Restricted data may only be stored in the approved data platform and must never be copied to personal devices, USB drives, or third-party AI tools.

## Using generative AI tools

Employees may use the company-approved AI assistant for Public and Internal information. Confidential or Restricted information must not be pasted into any external AI tool. Code generated by AI must be reviewed by a person before it is merged.

## Phishing

Report suspicious emails with the "Report phish" button in the mail client. Security runs a phishing simulation every quarter.

## Security incidents

A security incident is any event that may compromise the confidentiality, integrity or availability of company data. Report incidents immediately to security@halcyon.example. Do not attempt to investigate on your own.
