# Health, Safety and Flight Operations

> Synthetic demo document for a fictional company.

## Flight tests

Every drone flight test requires a signed risk assessment, a qualified pilot in command and a visual observer. Flights are not allowed when sustained wind speed exceeds 38 km/h or gusts exceed 50 km/h.

Test flights over public roads are never permitted.

## Field site visits

Staff visiting a wind farm or bridge site must wear a helmet, safety boots, high-visibility clothing and, above 2 metres, a fall-arrest harness. Lone working at field sites is prohibited: at least two people must be on site.

## Batteries

Lithium-polymer drone batteries are stored in fire-resistant bags at 40–60% charge. Damaged or swollen batteries are placed in the sand bin in the test hangar and reported to the hangar lead.

## Reporting accidents

All accidents and near misses are reported in the SafeTrack app within 24 hours. Serious injuries are reported to the Health and Safety Authority by the Head of People.

## First aid

Trained first aiders are listed on the noticeboard at each office. First aid kits are located at reception and in the test hangar.
