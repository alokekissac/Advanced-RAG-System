# Halcyon Robotics: Company Overview

> Synthetic demo document. Halcyon Robotics is a fictional company created for this project.

## Who we are

Halcyon Robotics Ltd. designs autonomous inspection drones for wind farms and bridges. The company was founded in 2019 and is headquartered at 14 Grand Canal Quay, Dublin 2, Ireland. A second engineering office opened in Cork in 2023.

As of 2026 Halcyon employs 142 people across four departments: Engineering, Field Operations, Commercial, and People & Finance.

## Core hours and office access

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00 Irish time, Monday to Thursday. Friday is a meeting-free focus day.

The Dublin office is open from 07:00 to 21:00 on weekdays. Staff badges do not open the office at weekends unless a weekend access request has been approved by a department head at least two working days in advance.

## Our values

1. Safety before speed: no flight test goes ahead without a signed risk assessment.
2. Write it down: decisions are recorded in the team decision log.
3. Leave the site better than we found it.
